Who We Are

Trusted IT partner since 2010.

OctaUnitech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a Bangalore-based technology company focused on cloud migration, software development, digital transformation, and system integration.

We have been a trusted partner to small, medium, and large enterprises—including Fortune 500 firms—helping businesses overcome critical challenges through integrated, reliable, and cost-effective IT solutions.

Our aim is simple: help organisations transform their IT environment with solutions that provide a lasting competitive edge. With our registered office and development centre in Bangalore, we support clients across geographies.

  • AWS, Azure & Google Cloud certified
  • ISO 27001 compliant & CMMI Level 3
  • End-to-end ownership — strategy to support
  • Serving Fortune 500 & SMEs globally
